## Trace a new feature

Start at the route in `src/pages/`, locate the component's dispatch/call, inspect the thunk in its slice (or direct `axiosRequest` call), inspect the interceptors for auth and scope behavior, then inspect permission gating in the page and `permissionPageMap`. Check a success response and each error case in DevTools Network. Keep a test case for a different role and a different unit, not just the happy-path admin.

## Template and unverified surfaces

`src/pages/apps/`, `src/pages/components/`, `src/pages/charts/`, `src/pages/forms/`, `src/pages/ui/`, `src/pages/tables/` and many `src/pages/pages/` routes are template/demo surfaces. Presence in the repo is not evidence of a released HRMS capability. `/sites`, `/overtime`, `/biometric` and selected admin features are page surfaces; check backend enablement and product ownership before promising them. A few backup-named page files exist; inspect Next route discovery before assuming they are inactive.
